The honest review

Where the plan is strong, and where it can quietly kill you.

Vision coherence is rare

Every use case collapses to one insight: intelligence over inventory. That's a real moat, not a slogan. Most founders never get this clean.

You have working proofs

Batman Hunt, Pokémon runs, the CLZ parser across ~2,700 comics. You're past "is this valuable?" — that's a huge de-risk.

One backend, many faces

IQVault + VaultOS on shared services is the correct architecture. It's leverage instead of duplicated work.

Provenance discipline

"NM assumed" beating silent fill-in is a mature instinct most teams learn the hard way. Bake it into the schema on day one.

Scope is the real enemy

Glasses, grading ML, custom models, POS — each is a company. The freeze isn't a suggestion; it's survival. Backlog everything not in the active milestone.

Data rights are fragile

Comps and pop reports live behind terms and rate limits. Treat every source as a swappable adapter or one dead scraper stalls the product.

Agents before contracts = chaos

Orchastr8 is powerful but hallucination-prone. No agent ships without a contract, evidence bundle, and a critic on high-dollar calls.

False precision erodes trust

"$412" is a lie; "$340–$460, 3 matched sales, medium confidence" is a product. Ranges and evidence counts, always.

01

The Frozen Scope

Twelve commitments. Nothing outside this box gets built until a milestone gate opens it.

❄ Scope Locked These are binding. New ideas go to the backlog, not the build queue.
F-01
Platform, not app.

Build VIP as a shared intelligence core. IQVault and VaultOS are role-specific faces on one backend — never forked products.

F-02
One canonical data model.

Assets, events, market evidence, signals, theses, recommendations — one truth, with vertical extensions.

F-03
Decisions over inventory.

Every feature must produce an action: Buy / Hold / Grade / Sell / Lot / Pass — with confidence and reasons.

F-04
Evidence & provenance are mandatory.

Field-level source, method, model version, and confidence. Inferred values never masquerade as verified facts.

F-05
Raw imports are immutable.

Keep every source snapshot forever. Processed outputs are always regenerable from originals.

F-06
IQVault is the proving ground.

Your personal edition validates every reusable module before it reaches paying users.

F-07
Generalize the proven three.

Collection Hunts, Pokémon intelligence runs, and comic ingestion become reusable services — not one-offs.

F-08
Automated signals + prediction scoring.

No manual news pulls. Ingest on a schedule, quarantine noise, and grade predictions after the fact.

F-09
Contracted agents only.

Every Orchastr8 agent has a mission, tools, output schema, confidence rules, and a critic for high-dollar calls.

F-10
Phone before glasses.

Decision Lens ships on the phone first. Glasses consume an already-validated workflow — never lead with hardware.

F-11
Imaging is a measurement system.

Grading capture means calibration, QA, and provenance — not a cosmetic photo booth.

F-12
Freeze holds until gates pass.

Each milestone's acceptance tests must pass before the next major concept unlocks.

❄ Parked until a gate unlocks it

  • AI glasses / wearable interface
  • PSA→CGC/TAG crossover ML model
  • Full POS & event management
  • Marketplace listing automation
  • Custom / unsupervised model training
  • Every collectible category at once
  • Final legal names, domains, trademarks
  • Premium data-feed marketplace

Decision Engine v0.1

The part that makes VIP worth paying for.
NEXT

  • All-in cost · market range · liquidity · target price

  • Configurable constraints: budget, risk, horizon, goals, premium tolerance

03

The first true MVP

The single sentence you're allowed to be obsessed with.

A collector imports a collection, scans or searches an item, sees a credible evidence-backed market range, understands liquidity and collection fit, gets a Buy / Hold / Grade / Sell / Pass call, and saves the decision with its evidence and outcome.

The same engine later reframes that recommendation for an LGS as a max buy offer and expected margin. Everything else — POS, marketplace automation, grading ML, glasses, every category — waits. The thesis is validated only when you repeatedly trust and act on the decisions.
