How to price sealed product against the secondary market

One pager for the counter. dealer-kit@0.1.0.

  1. Invoice + freight + shrink is the floor. Secondary mid is not a cost.
  2. If secondary high < floor, you do not have a product — you have a lot. Liquidate or do not buy the case.
  3. Allocation week: price at secondary low + 5–10%, not MSRP, not eBay sold outliers.
  4. After week 6 of a TCG set, sealed that is still on the wall is inventory risk. Cut before the reprint rumor.
  5. Never use PriceCharting 'new' as a sealed sticker without checking eBay sold that morning.

Worked check: invoice $48 + freight $2 = $50 floor. eBay sold last week $52–$68. Sticker $64 (in band, 28% on list). If solds print $44, you do not have a product — you have a lot.
