How to read the Flip Score

One page. Print it. Tape it inside the show binder. dealer-kit@0.1.0.

70–100 Buy now
Ask at/under sold low. ≥3 comps. Liquidity not dead.
45–69 Hold
In band. Fine collection copy. Not a trade.
0–44 Pass
Over the high, no comps, or a named trap.

The number is not the decision

VIP still ends in Buy / Hold / Grade / Sell / Lot / Pass with reasons. Flip Score is the stripped front. A 90 with two comps is a vanity number.
