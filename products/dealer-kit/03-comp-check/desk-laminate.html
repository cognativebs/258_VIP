90-Second Comp Check

Laminate this. $12 field kit. If it takes longer than 90s you are researching, not buying.

  1. Photo the asking price and the item. Do not rely on memory.
  2. Search eBay Sold for the exact print / grade / cert — 30s.
  3. Confirm on 130point if the phone eBay app is lying to you — 20s.
  4. If slabbed: open the cert. If comic: check restoration tells. If TCG: name the print — 20s.
  5. Glance PriceCharting / GoCollect only to see if your sold band is insane — 10s.
  6. Three solds inside 90 days that beat the ask → consider. Otherwise walk.

Three traps

Regraded slab
Fresh label on an older cert, or cert lookup shows a prior grade / different grader.
Open the cert on PSA/CGC/BGS before money moves. No cert page → walk.
Doctored comic
Color touch on spin/logo that looks too saturated vs the rest of the cover.
Price as restored until a grader says otherwise. Restored comps are a different market.
Reprint / proxy TCG
Wrong back color, blurry holofoil, or a 1999 stamp on a card that should be shadowless / 1st.
If you cannot name the print (1st / shadowless / unlimited / revision) you cannot price it. Pass.
